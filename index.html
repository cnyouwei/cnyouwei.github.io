Biography

Wei You joined the Department of Industrial Engineering and Decision Analytics at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology in July 2019. Stochastic models, queueing theory, applied probability, sequential decision-making, and their applications to the performance analysis and design of service systems and digital experimentation. He received his Ph.D. degree in Operations Research (2019) from Columbia University, advised by Professor Ward Whitt, and his B.S. degree in Mathematics (2014) from Nanjing University, China.
