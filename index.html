About

Wei You is an assistant professor of Industrial Engineering and Decision Analytics at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. His research focuses on Stochastic models, queueing theory, applied probability, sequential decision-making, and their applications to the performance analysis and design of service systems and digital experimentation. Previously, he received his Ph.D. degree in Operations Research (2019) from Columbia University, advised by Professor Ward Whitt, and his B.S. degree in Mathematics (2014) from Nanjing University, China.
