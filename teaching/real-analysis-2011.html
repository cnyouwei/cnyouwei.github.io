Nanjing University, School of Mathematics

NJU: Real Analysis Notes

A set of real analysis notes originally written in Chinese at Nanjing University in 2011 and later translated into English, with context and commentary in a companion blog post.

All Course Pages Main Site Teaching Section

Course Snapshot

  • Institution: Nanjing University, School of Mathematics
  • Period: 2011
  • Audience: Undergraduate mathematics students and self-learners.
  • Prerequisites: Single-variable and multivariable calculus.

Learning Outcomes

  • Understand the measure-theoretic foundations behind modern real analysis.
  • Connect key results across topology, measure, integration, and differentiation.
  • Use the notes as a structured reference for study and teaching.
